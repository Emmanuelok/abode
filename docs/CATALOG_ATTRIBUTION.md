## Download provenance

The official source page, official root README, official listings README, official images README, and the license included in abo-listings.tar state CC BY 4.0. Those documents were retrieved on 2026-09-23. Older research papers and third-party registry descriptions sometimes refer to an earlier CC BY-NC release; this selection uses the current official CC BY 4.0 download release.

The complete original metadata archive contains 147,702 listings. The normalization pass identified 15,741 distinct eligible English-language household listings after filtering and deduplication. The selected 3,133 products have 3,133 distinct original listing IDs and 3,133 distinct main-image identities. No names, colors, descriptions, or images were multiplied to fabricate catalog size.

Quality review: three listings whose source main image showed only the AmazonBasics logo were removed. Every retained image decodes successfully, has a maximum side length of 600 pixels, and has a distinct file SHA-256 digest. A representative image contact sheet was visually inspected.
