# Catalog attribution

The current collection contains 5,357 unique named reference listings, including 5,349 selected from Amazon Berkeley Objects (ABO) and 8 individually attributed Wikimedia Commons reference records.

## Amazon Berkeley Objects
Product data and photographs © Amazon.com and the dataset contributors. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Dataset: https://amazon-berkeley-objects.s3.amazonaws.com/index.html
Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Original product identifiers, source listing URLs and original photograph URLs are retained in each record. Records were selected, classified, reformatted and crosslisted in relevant collections. Some duplicated source-locale titles were consolidated. Main photographs were resized and converted to WebP. Illustrative GHS planning estimates were added; they are not source retail prices.

## Additional reference images
Wikimedia Commons photographs and a public-domain wallpaper design fill specialist reference collections. Each product detail page includes the creator, source and licence link. Full attribution is also available at public/catalog/reference-image-credits.json. Converted CC BY-SA images retain their original share-alike licence. No manufacturer stock or sourcing relationship is implied.

## Editorial imagery
The living, dining, courtyard, bedroom, kitchen, bath and office room images were generated for Abode Ghana. These depict conceptual rooms and are not exact product bundles.

## Limits
All products are reference listings until verified supplier records replace them. No live Ghana stock, verified customer reviews, merchant pricing or product safety certification is claimed. Abode Ghana is not affiliated with Amazon, Wayfair or the reference brands.
